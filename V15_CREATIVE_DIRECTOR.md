# V15 Creative Director

V15 targets every V14 review category below 8/10: Hook, Caption/Motion Typography, Dynamic Editing, and Voice/Story Audio.

- Hook Director 3.0: 0.24–0.38s micro-promise, no long payoff spoiler.
- Shot Transformation Director: punch-in/push-out/detail emphasis plus compression of low-progress ranges.
- Motion Typography: 2–5 word cards that complement voice instead of repeating it.
- Real Vietnamese TTS: edge-tts neural voice, timeline placement, automatic music ducking, AAC final mix.
- Safe fallback: if TTS/network is unavailable, video still renders and reports fallback_no_tts instead of failing the whole edit.
